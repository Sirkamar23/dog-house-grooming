About us

Every pet is family here

At Dog House Grooming, our experienced groomers bring patience, a gentle touch and plenty of love to every appointment. Whether it's a fluffy pup, a shy cat or a chatty bird, we take our time, keep things calm and treat your pet just like one of our own. You'll pick up a clean, comfortable and happy companion, and we'll be glad to see you both again.
